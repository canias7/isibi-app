Manifesto

We don't ask permission.

Every piece starts on a wall somewhere at 3am. We photograph it, we print it heavy, and we make four of them. Not four hundred. When they're gone they're gone, and the wall gets painted over anyway.

No restocks. No influencer seeding. No "drop culture" nonsense beyond the only part that matters: if you weren't there, you missed it.

FAQ

The bits people ask.

How does sizing run?

Boxy. Everything is cut one size up on purpose — if you're between sizes, take the smaller one.

Will you restock?

No. Four of each, then the print is retired. The wall it came from usually is too.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere with a postcode. Tracked as standard, 2–5 days in the UK and EU.

Returns?

30 days, unworn, tags on. Send it back and we'll put it in front of the next person on the list.
