# 3D models for customers' sites (2026-10-22)

Decided by the owner (2026-10-22): when a customer asks for a 3D asset, unless
they ask otherwise, it is made with fal's Meshy 7.1 (textured, $1.20, no rigging
or animation) and shown with three.js through the kit's `Scene3D`.

Built on `claude/meshy-3d` (record: `docs/history/2026-10-22-meshy-3d.md`):
price shown first (150 credits, the cap), debit by ref before fal, refund by ref
on failure, fal's queue only, files kept in the site's uploads, Scene3D placed
on the asked page and published, reachable from the chat (router field
`model3d`) and directly (`/api/site/<slug>/model3d`).

## Open for the owner

1. **One paid run** on a test site (about 150 credits) to measure the real
   answer, timing and file sizes.
2. **The price**: 150 credits is fal's cost with no margin. Keep it, or add one.
3. **The switch**: on wherever `FAL_KEY` is set; `MODEL3D_PURCHASES=off`
   refuses. Should it start off on production, like `IMAGE_PURCHASES`?
4. **Polycount**: 12,000 triangles (smart topology). Lower for phones, or the
   15,000 ceiling for detail.
5. **Merge and deploy**, only on your word.
