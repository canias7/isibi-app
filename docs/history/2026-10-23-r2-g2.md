## For the owner

**A38-5 — old builds are never deleted under draft-first.** Every save stages
a full build (about 1 MB) and nothing prunes them, so storage grows and the
Versions list, Take offline and Back online get slower and eventually fail.
Options:
1. Prune at each Publish: keep the live version and the one before it, every
   unpublished revision, the last 20 published, and anything staged in the last
   hour (a job may still be using it). *Recommended.*
2. The same, from the nightly tick instead of Publish (no work on the click).
3. Keep everything and only make the readers page (storage keeps growing).

**S7-4 — a crashed job's half-saved change can be dropped by the next edit.**
While a dead job's turn still stands (up to 10 minutes), the next page edit
reads the editable copy unrepaired and saves over the dead job's change.
Options:
1. Make that edit wait for the turn (it starts later, up to the turn's lapse).
   *Recommended*: simplest and never loses a paid change.
2. Refuse with "another change is still finishing — try again in a minute".
3. Read the draft's own staged version when the copy is behind (no wait, more code).

**A11-7 — Publish may report "not yet certain" while the new script is still
spreading.** Plausible only; the 15 s figure was not a propagation
measurement. Options: wait for the first live Publish to measure (*recommended*),
or poll for up to 30–60 s before answering.
