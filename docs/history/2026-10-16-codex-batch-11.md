## 1. The defect (Codex's reproduction)

`runReviewReconcile` read the rows under review with `needs_review=true`,
ordered by `updated_at` ascending, limit 20. An unresolved job changes only
its schedule in R2, never its row. So the same 20 oldest rows held every
pass, even while deferred.

Codex used 21 review jobs: the oldest 20 with unreadable draft evidence,
the 21st with a valid saved draft. Nine passes never reached job 21, while
calling `reconcileEditJob` on it directly settled it as `draft-saved` at
once, without publishing.

## 2. The fix (`worker.js`, `runReviewReconcile`)

- **A durable keyset cursor** (`recovery/review-cursor.json`) holds the last
  row's `updated_at` and id. Each tick reads the next 20 rows after it, in
  (updated_at, id) order.
- **Equal timestamps** are ordered by id, so none is skipped or repeated,
  even when more rows share one timestamp than fit a page.
- **A short page is the end**: the cursor is removed and the next tick
  starts from the front. An unresolved row is visited again within
  ceil(rows / 20) ticks, and asked only when its schedule is due.
- **A row that leaves review, or whose timestamp moves on**, falls out of
  the walk, or back into it later.
- **Restarts**: the cursor survives them. A missing or unreadable cursor
  starts from the front, so nothing is lost, only re-read. A failed read
  keeps the cursor.
- **Nothing about a row is changed to move it**: its timestamp, billing and
  review state stay as they were until its own verdict decides them. The
  retry schedule, billing once, dependency waiting and explicit Publish are
  unchanged.
- `readEditRows` takes an order; the request-flow fixture answers the keyset
  filter and a two-key order as PostgREST does.

## 3. Tests

- **`test/review-traversal.test.mjs`** (RVT1–RVT4), through the real
  `runReviewReconcile` and `reconcileEditJob`, against real draft saves and
  a job table answered as PostgREST answers it. Edit and Add-on jobs are
  mixed.
  - **RVT1: Codex's case.** The 21st is reached on the second pass and kept
    once. The 20 stay scheduled and are never refunded. A duplicate pass
    asks nothing again. The walk wraps after its last page. Nothing goes
    live.
  - **RVT2: 37 jobs**, 27 of them sharing one timestamp (more than a page).
    Ties are answered in no promised order, and a row changes mid-walk.
    Every recoverable row is reached; one lap reads every row once, and none
    is reconciled twice.
  - **RVT3: a restart**, with the cursor gone or unreadable. Deferred rows
    are not asked early, and the recoverable row is still reached.
  - **RVT4: the evidence restored.** All 21 are kept once each within 12
    passes, never refunded, their schedules removed, and nothing goes live.
- **SRB7 completed.** It now also shows the recovery: the evidence restored,
  the next due pass keeps the job as a saved draft, committed once, and its
  schedule goes.
