## A37-2: the turn wait in the container

- **Checked again.** Batch 1 (`docs/history/2026-10-18-audit-batch-1.md` #3)
  and its review already did most of the fix. `takeTurn` has its own limit
  (`TURN_WAIT_MS` = 2 × `TURN_TTL_MS`, so `turn-timeout`). A 403 ends it at
  once (`turn-refused`). Five failed reads in a row end it as
  `turn-unreadable`. None of the three is in `OVERLAP_LOSSES`, so none is
  re-run, and none of their replies blames another change.
- **Still real:** the loop never read `env.JOB_STOP`. When the container
  stopped a job at its deadline, the job kept polling until the process
  exited, and then ended with no answer.
- **Fix** (`worker.js`, `takeTurn`): the loop checks `JOB_STOP` the same way
  `gate()` does, marks the job `stopped` and returns. `turnStop` answers
  `error: "stopped"` with the gate's own sentence.

## A01-4: Neon project recovery in the container

- **Real.** `neonTearingDown` reads `neon_teardown` with the service key.
  Through the gateway's Supabase wall that table was not admitted
  (`why: "table"`). `settleNote` therefore answered `teardown-unreadable`,
  and provisioning threw `reconcile_project` on every retry.
- **Fix** (`builder/job-gateway.mjs`): `neon_teardown` gets a GET rule of a
  new kind, `probe`. It admits exactly one shape:
  `project_id=eq.<a project id>&select=id&limit=1`, which is the query the
  Worker sends. Anything else on that table is refused, `why: "probe"`: no
  filter, another column, a list, `in.(…)`, `limit` other than 1, an extra
  parameter, or a repeated id. Every other method is refused as `method`.
  So a job can ask whether one project is going away, never which projects
  are. `test/sb-gateway.test.mjs`'s list of refused tables no longer names
  `neon_teardown`.
- **Not done:** treating a gateway 403 as a configuration alert (the audit's
  last suggestion). Kept separate.

## A05-2: what the turn wait answers

- **Checked again.** The terminal-error half was fixed in batch 1 (above).
- **Still real:**
  - a wait ended by the customer's cancel answered `turn`, an overlap loss,
    and was re-run;
  - the step that holds the turn from its start answered `turn` when it had
    the turn but the saved-state check (`ensureEditableState`) failed.
    `publishBeside` answers that same failure as `read`.
- **Fix** (`worker.js`):
  - a cancel during the wait marks the job `cancelled`, and `turnStop`
    answers `error: "cancelled"`, which the request reads as stopped;
  - the holds path answers `read` when the turn is held, not lost, and the
    check failed;
  - the holds path uses the stop's own sentence (`heldStop.msg`) when it has
    one.
- `turn` now means only one thing: a wait that ran out of the Worker's
  inline budget, or a turn that was lost. Those are still overlap losses.

## A05-3: a renewal bringing back a given-back turn

- **Real.** `renewTurn` checked only the owner, so a heartbeat that read the
  given-back record (`until: 0`) wrote it held for 10 more minutes.
  `giveTurn` ignored a refused put. Both give-back sites cleared the job's
  turn only after awaiting `giveTurn`.
- **Fix** (`worker.js`):
  - `renewTurn` answers false when `until` is not in the future;
  - `giveTurn` reads and puts up to twice, so a renewal that moved the etag
    between them does not leave the turn held;
  - the queued job's `finally` and `publishBeside` drop the job's turn before
    they await the give-back.

## A05-5: turn contention through the gateway

- **Real gap.** Before this branch, no test had two jobs contend for the
  turn through the gateway.
- **Added:** G6. Two jobs each hold their own `GatewayBucket`, signed with
  their own tokens, over one store. Every call goes through the real
  `gatewayHandler`. The first takes the turn. The second polls (several
  gateway GETs) and does not take it while the first holds and renews it. It
  takes the turn once the first gives it back, with a larger fence. The
  first's late renewal is refused. The wall refuses nothing.
- **Left:** a fired case with two concurrent `runContainerJob` calls, and a
  fenced write refused with 412 over the wire. G6 drives the turn's own
  functions, not whole jobs. DH6's title still says "beside each other".
