## What was wrong

The router's instructions make several changes in one message one `look`
answer, and name "the menu and the button" among what `look` works out. On
the look door the lane picker chooses lanes by their descriptions, and none
described the menu's items:
- the one lane whose rung is the menu editor (`action`, the only lane with
  `laneLayer` `nav`, so `doorLane("nav")`) said it was "The site's primary
  button in the header … Only that button, and only its label and link";
- `behavior` names a menu only as a control that DOES something.

**Reproduced through the real edit route with supplied answers**, sync and
queued, on run 47's stored pages (`test/fixtures/run47/`), with "Take Gallery
out of the menu and put the order band above the starter story." routed
`look`, the answer the router's instructions give:
- **with run 47's recorded reading** (the real picker placed "Take Gallery out
  of the menu." on `behavior`): the look step answered nothing, the menu
  editor never ran, Gallery stayed in every menu, the layout shipped alone,
  and the screen read "✅ Updated /. ⚠️ I couldn't work out how to change the
  site's look that way. Say which part — a colour, the fonts, a section — and
  what it should look like.";
- **with the menu words placed nowhere** (scratch, not committed): the layout
  shipped and the screen read "✅ Updated /.", the menu change dropped without
  a word. This holds for any lane a scoped answer leaves out, so it is
  recorded separately (backlog);
- **and when the picker named the menu editor's lane with the menu's words,
  the route already did the right thing**: the four route cases below pass on
  the unfixed code. A scoped non-page lane is dispatched with its own words
  (`ask`), and the step loop hands them to the rung as its instruction. The gap
  was only what the picker is told.

## What changed (`builder/site-lanes.mjs`, three strings and their comments)

- **`action`'s hint** now describes the menu's items as well as the button:
  *"The site's MENU and its header button, the same on every page: which pages
  the menu lists, in what order, under what names — adding, taking out or
  reordering an item — and the button's words and the page, number or address
  it links to. What any control DOES when used is `behavior`."*
- **`action`'s removal wording**, shown to the picker under `removes`: *"take
  an item out of the menu (its page stays on the site), or the header's button
  off altogether"*. Nothing reads a removal for this lane but the picker: the
  menu editor decides from its own words whether the button goes.
- **`behavior`'s border clause**: *"— even about the header button or the
  menu; their WORDS, LINKS and items are `action`."*
- **The comment and the dispatch table** say why.

**No new lane**: every lane is a field of the design tool
(`test/edit-lanes.test.mjs`, both directions), and the removal door needs
exactly one lane per rung (`doorLane`): a second lane leading to `nav` would
make `doorLane("nav")` null and the router's own menu step would stop being
inserted on its removal door. **No route change and no keyword rule**: the
menu editor already writes every page's menu, and a scoped answer already hands
it only the menu's words; the whole mixed message never reaches it on a scoped
answer.

**The picker's tool grew by 117 characters** (8,233 → 8,350). The size guard
(`pick + css < whole / 10`) reads 9,663 against 9,714.2; a first, longer
wording read 9,843 and was cut rather than the guard moved.

## Tests (`test/edit-removal-door.test.mjs`, section 5: 19 cases)

Every model answer is supplied. The executors act only on the words they are
really handed: the menu editor takes Gallery out only when its request's own
words mention Gallery and nothing of the layout; the page writer moves the band
only when shown the home page and handed the layout words and nothing of the
menu.
- **The picker is told where a menu's items go**, on the look door and on the
  removal door's `additional` list: the lane that runs the menu editor
  describes the menu and still the button, its removal says what happens to a
  menu item, and `behavior` names that lane.
- **Menu + layout on the look door** (sync and queued, with and without the
  lane marked as a removal): the calls are the picker, the page writer and the
  menu editor; the menu editor is handed exactly "Take Gallery out of the
  menu" and the page writer exactly the layout words on `index.tsx`; one
  compile; Gallery leaves every copy of the menu; outside the menus only the
  home page's layout moved; no page is removed or moved; 5 credits (3 and 2).
- **The gap, reproduced, and the limit** (sync and queued): run 47's
  `behavior` reading still sends the menu words to the look step, and the menu
  editor never runs. The route honours the lanes it is given; whether a real
  picker now names the menu's lane is not measured.
- **The menu half fails** (sync and queued): the menu editor answers nothing it
  can apply (`no-menu`, its own sentence, and "That part still cost 2
  credits"), or cannot be reached (`send`, no charge): the layout ships alone,
  every menu is as it was, and the screen reads "✅ Updated /. ⚠️ …".
- **The layout half fails** (sync and queued): the layout's scope names a page
  the site does not have (withheld, `page/no-page`, no writer call), or the
  page's writers find nothing to change (`page/no-change`): Gallery still leaves
  every menu, nothing else moves, and the screen reads "✅ Updated the menu on 4
  pages: Today's bake · The starter · Visit. ⚠️ …".
- **Ordinary edits still work** (sync and queued): the menu change alone on the
  look door (exactly Gallery leaves every menu, the menu editor's own
  sentence), and the button change alone through the same lane (only the
  header's button changes on every page; the band's own "Order a loaf" button
  and every menu stay). The router's own `nav` paths are the existing controls
  in the same file, unchanged and passing.
