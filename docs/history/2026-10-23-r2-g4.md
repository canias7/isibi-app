## #227 S2-3: an addition's schema guard lost to the part that needed it. Fixed

- **Re-verified, real.** `othersOpen` (`worker.js`) counted every other part
  not ended, so in "add a booking page, then link it from Visit" the blocked
  link part made the addition's schema write lose with `conflict`, and the
  serial re-run repeated its model calls.
- **Fix**: `othersOpenIn` (`builder/request.mjs`), asked by `othersOpen`:
  a `blocked` part that needs this part or follows it (`parent`), directly
  or through another such part, cannot start before it ends and is not
  counted. Running, ready, paused and independently blocked parts still
  count, and an unreadable record is still "open". Kept answers of an
  `overlap` job are still not replayed (left as it was).
- **Guard**: `test/addition-schema-guard.test.mjs` (S2-3 cases). Red check:
  the file fails on the old code. Sweep: 5 mutants (the blocked test, the
  parent clause, the transitive loop, the terminal test, the old wiring)
  killed; the comment-only control survived.

## #245 A02-3: a routing job could hold the turn. Fixed

- **Re-verified.** The unbounded wait is gone (above), but a routing job whose
  request record read failed still kept `holds: true`, waited for the turn
  and held it through its model call.
- **Fix** (`worker.js`, `runQueuedSiteEdit`): a job whose stored address is
  `/api/site/route` sets `holds` and `schema` false before any record read,
  and skips the read.
- **Guard**: the A02-3 case in `test/addition-schema-guard.test.mjs`
  (structural: the routing test, before and instead of the read). Red check
  on the old file; sweep 2 mutants killed, control survived. No behavioral
  case: driving a routing job with an unreadable record needs a refused
  request key through the gateway, which also stops the routing's own save.

## #260 S1-7: a resumed part cannot read the paused job's checkpoint in the container. Needs the owner

Real: the fallback read `jobs/prepared/<paused>.held.json` is refused by
`allowedJobKey` (another job's id), so it only ever works on the Worker; the
staged copy, made on every claim, is what the container uses. Options:

1. **Leave it**: staging runs at every claim and a recovering part is refiled
   at once, so only lasting Worker read failures loop. Cheapest.
2. **Have the Worker retry staging and file nothing until it lands**, with a
   gateway refusal reported under its own reason (recommended: no wider
   gateway scope).
3. Admit the read in the gateway when the other job belongs to the same
   request, checked against the request record (widens the container's scope).

## #261 S2-4: the gateway path is proven only for a text part beside a data part. Needs the owner

Real: no gateway case drives a step holding the turn from its start (look,
logo, a serial retry), an overlap retry in the container, or a stop during a
turn wait. This is new test work. Options:

1. **Add one DH case**: a look part beside a text part through the real
   gateway (turn from start, fence stamp, publish). Recommended.
2. Option 1 plus a forced `conflict` serial retry and a stop during a wait.
3. Leave it.
