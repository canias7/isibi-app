## A10-3: a held replacement was paired only on the finish's own success

- **Re-verified.** The pairing ran only when `complete` answered 2xx. A failed
  draft seal answered 503, the client settled through the status read, and
  status never paired, so the panel said "Replaced" with no held change. A
  repeated finish after a pairing answered plain, because the record was
  deleted.
- **Fix** (`worker.js`, the media route). Pairing runs wherever the upload is
  seen finished: the finish, a repeated finish, the status read and a cancel
  that finds it finished. The `replacing/<sid>` record is kept, marked
  `paired` with its revision, so a later sighting answers `held` again and
  never holds the pair twice. Status and cancel carry `held` inside `file`.
- **Test** MR4c (`test/media-reliable.test.mjs`, the real routes): the
  finish's draft save fails (503 `draft`), the status read pairs it and says
  held, a repeated finish and status say held, `unpublishedOps` counts one
  `media-replace`, and the preview plays the new bytes.
- **Red check.** Fails on the old route (status not held). **Sweep** 5 of 5
  killed (status never pairs, pairs twice, paired record ignored, record
  deleted instead of marked, held put flat on status); comment control
  survived.
- **Not done.** A `replacing/` record whose upload was never finished is still
  not swept (small; deleted with the site by `clearSiteDraft`). Seen: a failed
  seal leaves its held journal file behind, unreferenced by the draft (the
  readers ignore it).

Still real (`public/chat.js`: drafts go to `pendingMedia`, sent only by a
build body). **Owner's choice:**

   send.

## A10-5: under draft-first, held deletes still count against the limits

- **Re-verified.** `used()` counts every stored file; a held delete keeps its
  room until Publish, and the refusal said "delete some first".
- **Fix (wording only).** Under draft-first on a published site, a `full`
  refusal says files deleted or replaced in the draft count until Publish; a
  replacement at the file limit is told it needs room for one more file
  (it waits as a separate file until Publish).
- **Test** MR4b: plain refusal off draft-first; held, the new wording and no
  "delete some first"; a replacement gets its own sentence. **Red check**
  fails on the old route. **Sweep** 7 of 7 killed across both commits;
  comment controls survived.
- **Owner's choice** for what counts: (1) leave as is with the new wording
  (recommended for now: storage never exceeds the cap); (2) subtract files
  held for deletion when admitting, so room frees at once (storage can exceed
  the cap until Publish); (3) count a held replacement as a replacement
  (files 0), so a full site can still replace a file.

## A11-5 and A11-6: the offline flag and recovery under draft-first

Both still real (`siteOffline` reads `site_builds`; `markSiteOffline` is
called only by the offline route; the publish recovery and script sweep
re-upload the script and recreate `site.live` without reading `offline_at`).
They turn on one product question: what "offline" means once Publish exists.
**Owner's choice:**

1. Offline wins until the owner presses Back online: Publish, recovery and the
   script sweep never bring an offline site back; taking a site offline
   retires any open publication journal and its script notes (the owner is
   told the publish was abandoned); a revise saves a draft and puts up no
   stand-in. **Recommended.**
2. Publish means "go live": Publish (and restore) clears `offline_at` when it
   creates `site.live`; only the background sweeps are stopped from bringing
   an offline site back.
3. Taking a site offline is refused while a publish is open ("finish or
   cancel the publish first").

Either way the flag should be read from where the live state changes, not
from `site_builds`.
