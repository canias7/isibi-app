  call (run 107: 3 each, uncached, about 8,600 tokens in with 512 cached);
- the routing answer may then send the part to a step that hands it on.

**Necessary calls versus avoidable duplication:**

  1's link needs the page, and routed after it, it sees the site as it is,
  with `/allergens` in the page list. The menu step's own check is needed too:
  it is what found the link present and charged nothing.

  cost the customer anything. The 3 credits each went to the routing call
  itself. For the TikTok part, which depended on nothing, the message's own

  and puts the rest in `alsoAsked` as words. Routing every part in one call is
  a router change (the message-level answer carrying a route for each held
  part, still re-checked after a dependency). **Recorded as an option, not
  made.**

  answer, model behaviour), then handed to the menu step. The add-on picker's
  call and about 23 s each are the cost of that misroute; the customer is not
  charged for it.
- **No pricing change and no keyword routing**: none made, none proposed.
- **A note on the 3-credit price**: these routing calls ran almost uncached
  (512 tokens of about 8,600 read from the cache). Message-level routing has
  cost 1 when cached (message 2's, row 405). Better caching of the part-level
  prompt would lower it without any pricing change. Not investigated further
