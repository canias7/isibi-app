## 1. What changed

- **`If-Range` on hosted video and audio** (`site-media.mjs` `ifRangeHolds`,
  the `/u/` route in `worker.js`).
  - The range stands only for a strong tag equal to the file's, or for the
    file's own upload date. A weak tag, another tag, a stale date or
    nonsense answers the whole current file (200).
  - The byte read is now conditional on the etag of the head it follows. A
    replacement landing between the two is answered from the new file
    whole, under its own tag, never as a range of it under the old length
    and tag.
- **`/u/<slug>` is tied to its host.**
  - A site's own label, its current name (a renamed site) and its custom
    domain serve only that site's uploads.
  - The app's host keeps serving every site's uploads publicly.
  - A domain that maps to no site serves none.
  - The preview already served only its own site's.
- **The media sweep's storage calls are metered.**
  - Every R2 call (listings, reads, heads, writes, deletes, multipart
    aborts) is counted against a share per prefix: sessions 120, parts 40,
    drafts 39, the cursor 1, which is 200 per tick.
  - A prefix stops *before* the item that would pass its share and keeps its
    place at the last key fully handled.
  - That item is redone from the start next lap; every step of it is
    idempotent.
  - The sessions cannot starve the parts and the drafts. Nothing under
    `uploads/` is ever deleted, and an unexpired session is never touched.
- **Unresolved recovery waits on a durable schedule.**
  - Two cases are covered: a lost build whose verdict is unknown (its
    pending entry's metadata), and a job under review the reconcile cannot
    decide (`recovery/review/<id>.json`).
  - Each unresolved pass doubles the wait: 2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 minutes,
    capped.
  - The count only spaces the asking. No number of tries settles anything as
    failed, refunded or published; what waits on it keeps waiting.
  - The first due pass whose evidence reads decides it, and a decided job's
    schedule is removed.
  - A decided outcome still being delivered (its answer write failed) is
    retried on the next tick, as before.
