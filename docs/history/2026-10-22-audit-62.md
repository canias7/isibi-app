# Audit finding 62 (A37-1), 2026-10-22

From `docs/audits/2026-10-17-builder-audit.md`, on branch `audit-62` (from

## The finding, checked again today

- **Real.** With the request flow on, an approved rewrite runs from
  `rewriteJob` (`worker.js`), whose body was `{ slug, instruction:
  p.resume || p.words, picker, effort, images, request }`, with no
  `alsoAsked` and no `handOver`. A step that climbs and puts a part off on the
  way (the look door's `elsewhere`, its deferred additions) carves that part
  into its own part (`carveParts`, `builder/request.mjs`), which adds the
  words to `parent.held` but leaves them in the parent's `words`.
  `askApproval` then sets `p.route = null`, so why the step climbed was lost.
- **Reproduced through the real request flow** (`test/request-flow.test.mjs`,
  "A37-1 — an approved rewrite is given the parts put off…"): "Turn this into
  a booking tool instead of a shop, and add a gallery page." The look door
  picks `kind` and names "add a gallery page" as `elsewhere`; `kind` climbs to
  the rewrite. Part 0 waits for the go-ahead with the whole sentence; part 1
  (the gallery page) waits on part 0. After the press, the stored rewrite job
  had no `alsoAsked`, and the rewrite's designer was handed "add a gallery
  page" (measured: with only `alsoAsked` removed from the fix, the case fails
  on "the rewrite's designer was handed the part put off"). Then part 1 ran
  the add-on: the page written twice and paid for twice.

## The fix

- **`builder/request.mjs`.** The hand-over reading `handedRoute` already made
  (`from`, `reason`, `field`, `page`) is now `handOverOf`, used by both. When a
  climb ends in the rewrite, the hand-over is read before `askApproval` clears
  the route and kept on the part (`p.handOver`). New `rewriteWire(rec, n)`:
  the part's held parts that are in what the rewrite runs (`alsoAsked`, as
  `jobBody` filters them, through `heldWire`) and the kept hand-over.
- **`worker.js`.** `rewriteJob` adds `...rewriteWire(rec, n)` to the body. The
  build route already reads both (`buildHeld` takes `alsoAsked` out of the
  instruction before anything reads it, or refuses at no cost; `readHandOver`
  gives the page writer its one line), as for the page's own climb
  (`EditPoll.handOver` into the rewrite).
- Not changed: A37-3 (the rewrite's 4,000-character read) stays open.
