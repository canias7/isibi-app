primitives, the vertical software surfaces they compose into, the builder/frontend, and the
platform infrastructure. Grouped by domain; each item is a concrete unit of work. Not prioritized
(that's a separate pass). Generated 2026-07-22.

> table-schema flags; **(ui)** are builder/generator or frontend capabilities; **(infra)** are
> platform/ops; **(vert)** are vertical-specific features composed from primitives.
