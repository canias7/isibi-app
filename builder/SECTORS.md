# Reference apps — the sectors

The industry sectors a reference app gets built for. Owner-authored, 2026-08-01.

Deliberately **not** organised by layout family. A sector is what a customer
says they are in; the arrangement is a separate decision made downstream.

A sector sits one level above a business: `builder/BUSINESSES.md` names 207
individual trades, and each of those belongs to exactly one sector here.
