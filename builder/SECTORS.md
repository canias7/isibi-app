# Reference apps — the sectors

The 500 sectors a reference app gets built for. Owner-authored, 2026-08-01.

A flat list, deliberately. Not grouped, and **not organised by layout family** —
a sector is what a customer says they are in, and the arrangement is a separate
decision made downstream.

A sector sits one level above a business: `builder/BUSINESSES.md` names 207
individual trades, and each belongs to a sector here.

**Granularity is not uniform, and that is deliberate.** 1–100 are the broad
sectors written first; 101–500 are specialisations of them, added after. So
`1. Retail` and `147. Afro & textured hair` sit in the same list at different
depths — the broad entry is the catch-all for anything its specialisations do
not name.
