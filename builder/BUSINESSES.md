# Reference apps — the businesses

The list of businesses a reference app gets built for. Owner-authored, 2026-08-01.

Deliberately **not** organised by layout family. A business is what a customer
says they have; the arrangement is a separate decision made downstream.

**207 businesses across 17 sectors.**

`✦` marks a business with no entry in the 165 trade names already carried by
`site-layouts.mjs` — **116 of the 207** below. The other 91 are already named
somewhere in that list, though naming one is not the same as having a reference
app for it: today there are 24 reference apps, one per layout family.
